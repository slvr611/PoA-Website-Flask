1Nation page rework — three directions
Assumptions & reasoning
  • All data stays — the 13 collapsible sections are regrouped into 6 domains: Overview, Economy (resources+jobs+trade), Development (districts+cities+wonders+tech+quests), Military, Diplomacy (relations+vassals), Laws. Nothing is removed, just relocated.
  • Header decluttered — Edit/Customize stay as buttons; pending changes, history, recalculate, and the admin visibility toggle collapse into one "⋯ More" menu. A pending-changes count badge replaces the always-visible link.
  • Your top tasks get a summary strip — resources/income, pops/jobs, stability and treasury are always visible above the fold, with breakdown tooltips kept (shown as dotted-underline numbers).
  • Per-nation flavor kept — accent color, flag and banner slots survive in all three (try the accent tweak ↗).
  • New tech view — a tier-by-tier list with progress bars replaces the Cytoscape graph as default; works at phone width (see Development in 1a).
  • Data shown is sample data for a fictional nation, Thornevale. Compare against Current Nation Page.dc.html (the baseline recreation).

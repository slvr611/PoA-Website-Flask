Fix City Placement

Enforces a minimum distance of 3 tiles between every pair of cities on the map, regardless of owner. AI-owned cities that violate this are relocated using the same node/admin-range scoring the AI uses when building a new city; if no legal tile exists anywhere for it, the city is removed instead — cleared from both the map and the nation's city list, with its build cost refunded back into the nation's resources. Player-owned cities are never moved or removed, only flagged below for manual review. Afterward, each AI nation's capital is moved onto whichever of its own cities is most central (preferring one with a node), and any duplicate or orphaned capital flags are cleared. Player nations' capitals are never touched.

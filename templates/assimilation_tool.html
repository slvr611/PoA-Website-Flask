Assimilation Tool

Merges one nation's districts, cities, and pops into another nation — e.g. after a war of conquest. The source nation is left with none of the transferred items; it is not otherwise modified or removed.

Source and target must be different nations.

After executing

  1. Open the map, enter Edit Mode, and repaint {{ source_nation.name }}'s former tiles as belonging to {{ target_nation.name }}.
  2. Run Tools ▾ → Sync Territory on the map so both nations' territory data is recounted.
  3. Check {{ target_nation.name }}'s jobs — pops that worked a district-gated job may have lost that assignment if the district itself didn't transfer with them.

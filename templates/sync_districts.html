Sync Districts

Reconciles AI nations' district lists against districts placed on the hex map. Districts found on a tile but missing from the nation page get added to the nation. Districts on the nation page that aren't placed on the map get placed using the AI's district placement logic. Existing districts and tiles are never modified or removed — only additions are made. Player-controlled nations, nomadic nations, and legacy (non-map) districts are not affected.
